# Limits of Cognition

The famous quote of Wittgenstein "The limits of my language mean the limits of my world." emphasizes the power language has on our cognition. But is it truly the limit of our cognition? Einstein is famously known for his thought experiments where he imagined himself flying along a light beam. So can our imagination transcend the limits of our thoughts in language?
So cognition is not limited by language, but cognition (and thus also language) is limited by imagination?

What is imagination then? Intuitive imagination happens in spacetime (3 space dimension and 1 time dimension) with a couple of dozens objects for the average human brain.

Can imagination actually produce knowledge before language, mathematics or formal reasoning can explain it?

Language forces continuous experience into discrete categories.

Language is abstract and compresses perception. So it always is a learned projection of our perception.
